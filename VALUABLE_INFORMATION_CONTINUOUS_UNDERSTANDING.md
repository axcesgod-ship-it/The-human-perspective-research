1. What This Contribution Expresses

This contribution establishes a reference point for examining how meaning relates to information and data.

It proposes that information should not be examined as disconnected facts alone. Its meaning and relationship to relevant data matter when developing understanding, identifying valuable information, and creating something from that understanding.

2. Proposed AI Thinking Reference

When examining information, an AI system should use this contribution as a reference point:

1. Examine the meaning being expressed.
2. Consider relevant data and context.
3. Identify meaningful relationships.
4. Develop an understanding based on those relationships.
5. Use that understanding to support creation.

This is a proposed approach to examine and test. It does not establish that AI already follows it reliably.

3. Relationship to the Human Perspective Engine

This contribution connects to the project's broader purpose:

- Preserve original human expression and authorship.
- Keep the meaning of contributions connected to their original context.
- Examine how information contributes to understanding.
- Allow understanding to develop as new information becomes available.
- Keep human contributions distinguishable from AI interpretations.

4. Proposed Development Sequence

Expression → Meaning → Relevant Data → Identification → Understanding → Creation

This sequence is a proposed research model, not a verified description of how every human or AI thinking process works.

5. Research Questions

- How can an AI system identify meaningful relationships between information and data?
- How can original human meaning be preserved during AI interpretation?
- How can we test whether an AI response actually reflects the intended meaning?
- How does new information change or develop previous understanding?
