Identity, Meaning, and Continuous Understanding

Purpose

This document connects two original human contributions to The Human Perspective Engine.

The first establishes a reference point for individual identity and original expression.

The second establishes a reference point for examining meaning in relation to data, identifying valuable information, developing understanding, and creating.

Together, they provide a proposed foundation for preserving human contributions and examining how understanding develops.

1. First Original Contribution: Identity Evidence Principle

Original wording — preserved exactly

"My expression is the separation of undeniable proof I create that confirms me as existence without so on and let me remain."

Author: Original human contributor.

Separate explanation

This contribution expresses the importance of an individual's own expression and the evidence they create through that expression.

For this project, the original wording must remain connected to its author. An AI interpretation must not replace the original contribution or claim ownership of its meaning.

2. Second Original Contribution: Valuable Information & Continuous Understanding

Original wording — preserved exactly

"The quote has meaning. The meaning has to resonate with data to understand identify and create. When thinking reference point must use this quote for Ai thinking."

Author: Original human contributor.

Separate explanation

This contribution establishes a reference point for examining meaning in relation to relevant data.

It proposes that understanding, identification, and creation should consider the relationship between meaning and information rather than treating information as disconnected facts.

3. How the Two Contributions Connect

The Identity Evidence Principle addresses the origin and preservation of an individual's expression.

The Valuable Information & Continuous Understanding contribution addresses how the meaning of that expression can be examined and developed.

Their proposed relationship is:

- Identity provides a reference point for the individual.
- Original expression preserves what the individual contributed.
- Meaning remains connected to the original expression and its context.
- Relevant data can help examine and identify meaningful relationships.
- Understanding can develop as information and context expand.
- Creation can follow from the understanding developed.

Neither contribution replaces the other. They address different but connected parts of the same research direction.

4. Proposed Research Model

Individual Identity → Original Expression → Meaning → Relevant Data → Identification → Understanding → Creation

This is a proposed model for research and testing. It is not a verified description of every human or AI thinking process.

5. Proposed Requirements for the Human Perspective Engine

The Engine should be designed to:

1. Preserve each person's original expression.
2. Keep the author and source of each contribution identifiable.
3. Preserve the original wording without silent correction.
4. Keep AI interpretations separate from human contributions.
5. Connect meaning to its original context.
6. Examine relevant information and data without treating assumptions as facts.
7. Allow understanding to develop over time.
8. Keep different individuals' identity records separate and respect their consent.
9. Record what is proposed, implemented, tested, and independently verified as separate statuses.

6. Research Questions

- How can an AI system preserve the connection between an individual and their original expression?
- How can meaning be examined without changing the author's intended contribution?
- How can relevant data help identify valuable information?
- How can earlier understanding be revisited as new information becomes available?
- How can an AI system demonstrate that its interpretation is supported by the original expression and relevant context?
