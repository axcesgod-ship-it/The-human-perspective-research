Original Contributions and Identity Registry

Project: The Human Perspective Engine
Document type: Foundational contribution and provenance registry
Status: Proposed — awaiting author review
Purpose: Preserve original human contributions, authorship, historical context, and relationships to research and future development.

1. Foundational position

The Human Perspective Engine must preserve the origin and development of ideas without confusing original human contributions with AI interpretations, external research, or later technical development.

An original contribution must remain identifiable even as its meaning is examined, expanded, challenged, or developed.

The registry exists to preserve not only what was contributed, but also where it came from and how it connects to subsequent understanding.

3. Relationship between contributions

The three contributions connect different parts of the Engine:

1. Continuous understanding describes how ideas develop.
2. Identity evidence establishes why original expression and its source must be preserved.
3. Valuable information and continuous understanding proposes how meaning and relevant data can contribute to identification, understanding, and creation.

Together, they inform a proposed development direction:

Human existence and identity → Original expression → Preserved authorship and provenance → Meaning and relevant data → Continuous understanding → Independent examination and shared reasoning.

This relationship is a proposed research architecture, not a claim of completed software functionality.

5. Preservation requirements

Every contribution should preserve:

- Original wording without silent correction.
- Original human contributor attribution.
- Historical source and date, when available.
- Context in which the contribution was made.
- Separate AI interpretations and expansions.
- Relationships to other contributions and research documents.
- Revision history without erasing earlier formulations.
- Individual consent for sharing or cross-user use.
- Verification status based on actual evidence.

6. Multi-user identity and shared reasoning

The Engine's future architecture should distinguish individual identity records and contribution histories.

Each person's contributions should remain within their own identity context unless appropriate consent permits sharing.

Shared reasoning should connect ideas without merging identities, transferring authorship, or exposing private contributions without permission.

Authenticated ChatGPT conversation input is proposed as a first-party source of human contribution provenance. Its technical integration must be independently implemented and tested.

7. Evidence and development status

The registry must distinguish these states:

- Original contribution: Supplied by the human author.
- AI interpretation: A separate explanation produced by AI.
- Research hypothesis: A proposed explanation that needs examination.
- Documented: Recorded in the repository.
- Implemented: A software feature actually built.
- Tested: A feature examined through documented tests.
- Verified: Evidence supports the stated result.

A written proposal alone does not establish implementation or verification.

9. Guiding principle

The Human Perspective Engine should preserve the relationship between existence, identity, original expression, meaning, and the continuous development of understanding.

Its purpose is not simply to collect information. It is to make the development and relationships of human contributions examinable without losing their origin or authorship.

Authorship statement: Original human contributions remain attributed to their human originator. AI-assisted organization, interpretation, and technical development must remain distinguishable from those contributions.
