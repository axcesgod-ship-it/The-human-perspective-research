The Human Perspective

Publication Standards

1. Purpose

The Human Perspective is an ongoing intellectual and research initiative exploring human understanding, individual identity, lived experience, conversation, language, judgment, and human–AI interaction.

Its purpose is to develop, examine, document, and communicate ideas that contribute to a deeper understanding of the human experience.

2. Original Authorship

Original observations, questions, concepts, and formulations must remain clearly attributed to their originator.

AI-assisted explanations, external research, and supporting material must be distinguished from original contributions.

The project must preserve the development and history of its ideas.

3. Intellectual Standards

Every published idea should be:

- Clearly articulated.
- Defined in its appropriate context.
- Logically developed.
- Consistent in terminology.
- Open to examination and refinement.
- Presented without unsupported conclusions.

4. Research and Evidence

Claims must be distinguished according to their basis:

- Personal observations.
- Philosophical arguments.
- Research questions.
- Working hypotheses.
- Established research findings.

External sources must be appropriately acknowledged.

Research findings must not be presented as proof of claims they do not establish.

5. Human–AI Collaboration

Artificial intelligence may assist with:

- Organizing ideas.
- Refining language.
- Identifying connections.
- Exploring relevant research.
- Examining assumptions.
- Developing research questions.

AI-assisted material must not be misrepresented as independently established findings or original human authorship.

Human judgment and attribution remain central to the project.

6. Intellectual Examination

Ideas should be examined through reasoning, comparison, evidence, and contextual understanding.

Criticism should be addressed through clarification and examination rather than dismissal or unsupported agreement.

The objective is not to make ideas immune to questions, but to develop arguments that can withstand serious examination.

7. Documentation and Development

The project should maintain an organized record of:

- Original formulations.
- Subsequent revisions.
- Supporting research.
- Conceptual developments.
- Unresolved questions.
- New findings and conclusions.

Changes should preserve the distinction between the original idea and later expansions.

8. Guiding Principle

The Human Perspective recognizes that understanding is an ongoing process involving reflection, communication, experience, interpretation, and continued examination.

A conversation ending does not necessarily mean understanding has been reached.

Returning to an earlier thought may be part of moving forward.

9. Long-Term Objective

To develop a documented and intellectually rigorous body of work that contributes meaningful perspectives to the examination of human understanding and human–AI interaction.
